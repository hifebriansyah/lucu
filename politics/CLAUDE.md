# politics/: book 2

100 Indonesian political jokes. The shared writing rules are in `../CLAUDE.md`. The cast uses the same names as book 1, but their backgrounds here are their own; don't carry over book-1 facts unless this file says so.

This book is about Indonesian politics, not about AI. Stories don't have to involve AI, and SITI appears only when the joke needs her; many stories should have no SITI at all.

Never name a real politician, party, region, or official. Satirize situations (kampanye, baliho, serangan fajar, bansos, pungli, janji pemilu), not real people.

## Parts

- Bagian Satu: Kampanye (1–20)
- Bagian Dua: Birokrasi (21–40)
- Bagian Tiga: Pesta Demokrasi (41–60)
- Bagian Empat: Proyek (61–80)
- Bagian Lima: Rumah dan Sekolah (81–100). Story 100 is the finale.
