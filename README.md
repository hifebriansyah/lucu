# lucu

Two books of short, funny Indonesian stories and a two-volume book of true stories, plus a small website to preview and print them (a coloring book is in progress locally).

## Books

| Folder | Title | Stories |
|---|---|---|
| `ai/` | *Kelakuan Pengguna Ai!!* | 62, in six parts plus an epilog |
| `politics/` | *Janji Tinggal Janji* (working title) | 100, in five parts |
| `bad/` | *Jahat Tapi Legal* (working title), two volumes | 33 true stories: Jilid 1 has 17, Jilid 2 has 16, each in three parts |

Book 1's author is Febriansyah, S.Kom., PSM, PSPO (the same name on the title page, imprint, Tentang Penulis and front cover); the manuscript ends with a Tentang Penulis page (page 149). The imprint page still has placeholders for the editor, cover designer, ISBN and publisher.

Book 3 is not a humor book. Its theme is the Dark Triad (narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy): true stories of people who got rich in legal but ruthless ways (Rockefeller's secret railroad rebates, Ray Kroc and the McDonald brothers, Jack Welch, Shkreli's Daraprim price, the EpiPen price, and more). Each story is about 5–7 A5 pages, in five sections: Kisahnya, Konsekuensi, Keuntungan, Analisa Dark Triad (how strongly each trait shows in the person's reported behavior, with a note that it isn't a diagnosis) and Hikmah (what to learn). A Mengenal Dark Triad page after the Kata Pengantar explains the three traits. It comes in two volumes, each its own book on the shelf (`reader.html?book=bad` and `?book=bad2`), with the same three sections: Zaman Dulu (before about 1990, Nusantara stories included: Raden Wijaya, Coen, Daendels, Raffles, Van den Bosch, Oei Tiong Ham, Liem Sioe Liong, plus Rockefeller, Rhodes, Kroc, Frick, Thomas Hicks, Kennedy, Welch, Arnault), Zaman Sekarang (Dunlap, Singer, Paulson, Shkreli, Thiel, BPPC, Sackler, Hicks & Gillett at Liverpool, Bresch) and Melewati Batas Legal, for people convicted of crimes (Ponzi, Leeson, Madoff, Holmes, eFishery, Belfort, Eddy Tansil, Skilling, Bankman-Fried). Each story is in `bad/stories/<slug>.md`; `VOLUMES` in `tools/naskah-bad.py` sets the order, and `python3 tools/naskah-bad.py` builds `bad/naskah.md` (Jilid 1) and `bad/naskah-2.md` (Jilid 2), numbering each volume from 1. The facts come from public reports with rounded figures. All 11 stories and the Dark Triad page were fact-checked on 24 November 2026, and the corrections are in; the sources are listed in `bad/SOURCES.md`. Check eFishery again before printing (its appeal ruling in July 2026 cut the sentence to six years, and a kasasi may follow). It has no cover picture yet, so the shelf shows the temporary cover; put them at `bad/cover.jpg` and `bad/cover-2.jpg`.

The story books have one story per file (`1.md`, `2.md`, ...) and a `naskah.md` with the full manuscript. Writing rules are in `CLAUDE.md`; each book's cast and background are in its own `CLAUDE.md`.

`ai/naskah.docx` is a Word export of book 1 for publishers and the ISBN submission: A5, 11 pt Literata, mirrored margins, parts on right-hand pages, running heads, page numbers and a Daftar Isi with page references (Word asks to update fields on open). It is generated from `naskah.md`, so rebuild it after the manuscript changes. The **Word** button on the print page builds the same file in the browser from the current `naskah.md` and downloads it as `<book>-naskah.docx` (`assets/docx.js`, with the docx and fflate libraries loaded from jsDelivr on first click), so it is always up to date and works for every book. It has the pictures too, where the paged book has them: each story's picture (and SITI's, for Kenalan dengan SITI) on the page after its text, with the text starting on a left-hand page; each part's picture as a full-page poster behind its white title; and the section art with its TRIVIA line on the left-hand page before the part. Word can't do the hexagon frames or rounded corners, so those pictures are plain rectangles. With Hitam putih on, the pictures go in grey. The file is about 18 MB (16 MB in grey). Install Literata on any computer that opens it.

## Preview site

Serve the folder with any static server and open `index.html`:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 5210 --directory lucu
```

- `index.html`: the bookshelf, with every book listed in `books.json`.
- `reader.html?book=<id>`: reads `naskah.md` as a paged book with a page-turn animation. The cover sits alone, then page 1 opens on the right. Arrow keys or swiping turn pages, and the dropdown jumps to any story. Buttons around the page number in the bar go to the previous or next page (‹ ›) and to the previous or next section (« »): each part, the titled pages outside the stories (Daftar Isi, Kata Pengantar, Kenalan dengan SITI, Penutup, Tentang Penulis) and the back cover; « goes to the start of the current section first. On phones they sit on a second row.
- **Hitam putih** in the reader bar turns every interior page black and white (pictures, toska frames and rules, chat bubble, part-title glow), for a grayscale print. The print page has the same button, and the choice is shared, so turning it on in either place prints the PDF in black and white (the info line says "hitam putih"). Each color swaps for a grey of the same brightness (`--siti-grey`, worked out from the chosen border color in `assets/paper.js`) instead of a grayscale filter over the page, because Chrome prints filtered text as a picture. The paper keeps its color and the cover stays in color. The browser remembers the choice.
- **Depan** and **Belakang** in the reader bar save the front and back covers as the reader shows them, text included, each as a PNG at A5 300 dpi (1748 × 2480 px, marked 300 dpi in the file so print apps open it at A5 size, `<book>-sampul-depan.png` and `<book>-sampul-belakang.png`). They are two buttons because browsers block a second download that starts seconds after one click. **Belakang** is hidden for a book without a back cover. **Punggung** saves the spine the same way (`<book>-punggung.png`): the spine's width depends on the page count and paper, so it is a full A5 picture of `ai/book-cover/side.jpeg` (copied to `web/side.jpg`, set by `sideCover`) with the title (KELAKUAN PENGGUNA Ai!!, white Orbitron with a cyan glow) down its exact center, reading top to bottom, with the kicker near the top in small light-blue Orbitron, upright and stacked two letters a line (SI / TI, then M4 / KZ / -A), in two columns so S T M K - and I I 4 Z A each line up. SITI's head and shoulders rise from the bottom edge of the spine, centered: `spineArt` in `books.json` points `tools/covers.py` at `ai/characterV3/siti.jpeg`, and the script cuts her out of its white background into `ai/book-cover/web/siti.png`. The title stays centered on the whole spine, clear of her, about 4.5 mm tall, so they fit a spine of 7 mm or more. The portrait is about 18 mm wide and dips about 6 mm below the edge, so a thinner spine shows only her face. The printer crops a strip of the spine's width around the center. It lays the cover out off screen and draws it with html-to-image, loaded from jsDelivr only when the button is used. The cover copy in `ai/book-cover/web/` is kept at full size (up to 2600 px tall) so the saved file stays sharp. A printer may want bleed around it; ask them for the exact size.
- **Kertas** in the reader bar sets the paper color of the pages: pick one of five book papers (Gading, the default, Krem, Putih, Novel, Abu) or any color from **Warna lain**, see it on the pages right away, then **Simpan** to keep it (closing without saving goes back to the saved color; **Default** returns to Gading). It is saved per book in this browser (`assets/paper.js`), and the print page uses it too, so the PDF comes out on that paper color, Gading unless another is saved. The same panel has **Bingkai gambar**, the color of the line around the story pictures: Toska (the default), Hitam, Abu, Emas, Merah or any color. **Simpan** keeps both colors, **Default** resets both, and the PDF uses the saved border color too. The button's dot shows the paper color with a ring in the border color. Its last section, **Bentuk gambar bagian**, picks the shape of the picture on the page facing each part picture: Hexagon (the default), 41-gon, Lingkaran, Persegi, Segitiga or 16:9 (Hexagon, Persegi and 16:9 get rounded corners of about 41px; the hexagon is drawn with a rounded-hexagon SVG mask, since `clip-path` can't round a polygon). Each button shows a small preview of its shape. It is saved with the colors and the PDF uses it too (`data-frame` on the page root, shapes in `assets/pages.css`).
- `print.html?book=<id>`: the print-ready interior, A5 (14,8 × 21 cm). It shows exactly the pages the reader shows: same page breaks, page numbers and contents page numbers. Both use one layout engine (`assets/paginate.js`) and one set of A5 page styles (`assets/pages.css`: 11 pt Literata on about 16 pt leading, Inter for page numbers and running heads, every page heading (story titles, Kata Pengantar, Kenalan dengan SITI, Daftar Isi and the part contents pages) in Orbitron like the part titles, centered with wide letter spacing, margins 2 cm top and bottom, 2 cm at the spine and 1.6 cm outside); the reader only scales the pages to fit the window. Use **Cetak / Simpan PDF** to save the PDF.

## Covers

Book 1 has a picture for every story, in both the reader and the print PDF. Every story starts on a left-hand page, and its picture fills the right-hand page facing it, inside the normal page margins (so it lines up with the text on the facing page), with 41px rounded corners, a 3 pt white gap, then a 1.5 pt toska border (`--siti` in `assets/pages.css`, changeable in the reader) and the page number below it. A story that runs over its page by fewer than 20 lines (Bawa Pantunnya, Gorengan, Draf, Strawberry) ends at the top of its picture page instead, and the picture shrinks to fill the rest, 7 mm below the last line (`SPILL_MAX` in `assets/paginate.js`). A story 20 or more lines over would continue on the next left-hand page, with a blank page facing it; no story does now. The book is 149 pages. The originals are `ai/story-cover/<n>.jpeg` (3:4, named by story number); the pages load the smaller copies in `ai/story-cover/web/<n>.jpg` (1200 px tall), set by `storyArt` in `books.json`. After adding, replacing or renaming any story or section picture, run `tools/covers.py` (see below).

Each part page is a full-page picture on a right-hand page, edge to edge, with a thin white line inside its edge (41px rounded corners, like the story pictures) and a poster-style title on top: the part name big (BAGIAN SATU, EPILOG) and its subtitle under it (JAM KERJA; the Epilog has none), in white Orbitron (a free Google font) with a cyan glow. The glow is a CSS `drop-shadow` filter rather than `text-shadow`, because Chrome prints a blurred text-shadow on outlined text as a solid box in the PDF. The pictures are `ai/section-cover/s1.jpeg` to `s7.jpeg`, in part order (s1 is Bagian Satu, s7 is the Epilog), with copies in `ai/section-cover/web/` set by `partArt`. If a story is renumbered, rename its picture too. The left-hand page before each part, which would otherwise be blank, shows a fan-service picture of SITI in a frame, a hexagon unless another shape is picked in the Kertas panel (a line in the picture-border color, a white gap, then the picture), facing the part's picture on the right (`partArtPage` in `assets/paginate.js`); Bagian Satu's is pages 10 and 11. The originals are `ai/section-art/1.jpeg` to `7`, in part order like the part pictures, with copies in `ai/section-art/web/` set by `contentsArt` in `books.json` and made by `tools/covers.py`. Under each picture is a line of trivia about Nadia, labeled TRIVIA in small Orbitron, from `contentsTrivia` in `books.json` (one line per part, in part order). The Word export has these pictures too.

Front-matter pages can have a picture too, set by `frontArt` in `books.json` (section heading → copy): "Kenalan dengan SITI" (page 8) faces a full picture of SITI on page 9, framed like a story picture. Page 8 is set in the middle of the page: its Nama / Jenis / Kode / Keahlian / Kelemahan lines become a profile card in toska (labels in small Orbitron), with the paragraph centered under it. Any front-matter paragraph made only of `**Label:** value` lines becomes such a card (`profileCards` in `assets/book.js`). The title page (page 1) sets the title like the cover, from `coverTitle`: KELAKUAN big and PENGGUNA Ai!! smaller, in Orbitron with plain dark ink so it prints cleanly, a short toska rule, the subtitle in italic Literata and the author in small Inter. Page 2, the imprint (any front section mentioning ISBN or Hak cipta), is set like a printed book's: small left-aligned type at the foot of the page, the book's name in Orbitron and a thin rule before the rights notice. Page 3, the dedication, is a short italic verse in the upper middle of the page; its closing question ("Yakin?") shows as a sent chat message under it, in a toska bubble marked Terkirim (`chatBubble` in `assets/book.js`; `naskah.md` is unchanged). Its original is `ai/front-art/siti.jpeg`, a link to `ai/characterV3/siti-cover.jpeg`, and `tools/covers.py` copies it to `ai/front-art/web/siti.jpg`, so after updating that picture just run the script.

`tools/covers.py` (needs Pillow) updates every copy in one go: the copies in `web/` that the pages load. There are no separate black-and-white copies: Hitam putih greys these same pictures in the browser, which keeps the site about half the size. By default it only redoes pictures that are new or changed, checked by their contents rather than file dates (a file copied in from Finder keeps its old date). It writes `web/index.json` with each picture's fingerprint, and the pages add it to the picture's address, so the browser never shows an old cached picture. It also deletes copies whose original is gone, and warns about a misnamed file (like `a4.jpeg`) or a story or section with no picture. Originals are never changed:

```bash
python3 tools/covers.py
```

```bash
python3 tools/covers.py --check
```

```bash
python3 tools/covers.py --all
```

Book 1 has a real front and back cover. The front is `ai/book-cover/front.jpeg` (or `cover.jpeg`), copied by `tools/covers.py` to `ai/book-cover/web/cover.jpg` (full size, up to 2600 px tall), which `cover` in `books.json` points to. The back is `ai/book-cover/back.jpeg` (any picture named `back`; the newest if there are several), copied to `ai/book-cover/web/back.jpg`, set by `backCover`. The reader ends the book with it: after the last page and the blank inside of the back cover, the back cover sits alone on the left, like the front cover starts the book alone on the right, and the contents dropdown lists it as Sampul belakang. With a spine picture (`sideCover`), the spine shows to the right of the back cover, as on the closed book seen from behind: a strip from the middle of the spine picture with its title and author (Punggung in the dropdown, `.spine-page` in `assets/reader.css`). The strip is 20% of the page wide (about 30 mm), wider than the real spine will likely be, since its width is not known yet. The picture has no lettering, so the pages set the back-cover text over it from `backText` in `books.json`: a two-paragraph blurb in the middle of the glowing circle, in white Inter with the cyan glow of the front cover's title, centered on `backCircle` (the circle's center in % of the picture, so move it if the picture changes), and the kicker at the foot; an optional `foot` line goes under it. There is no ISBN barcode yet; add one once the number is known. The picture has no lettering, so with `coverText: true` the pages set the title over it in the part pages' Orbitron poster style: the kicker (SITI · M4.KZ-A) on top, KELAKUAN big with PENGGUNA Ai!! smaller under it (`coverTitle` in `books.json`, written exactly as it should show), and the subtitle at the bottom with the author (`author`) under it, over dark fades. After replacing either cover, run `python3 tools/covers.py`. The other books' covers are placeholders; to use a real one, save it as `<book>/cover.jpg` (2:3, for example 1400 × 2100 px). The print PDF and the Word file are the interior only, and **Depan** / **Belakang** save the covers as PNGs; send both covers to the printer separately.

### Spot UV

The covers are meant to get spot UV. A spot UV mask is a second file the size of a cover: solid black where the printer adds the UV coating, white where it doesn't. There is one per cover piece, each saved by its own button as an A5 300 dpi PNG that lines up exactly with that cover's own file:

- **UV depan** (`<book>-sampul-depan-uv.png`): all the front cover's text (kicker, title, subtitle, author), SITI and the glowing circle behind the title (`uvMarkup`; the circle is an ellipse from `frontRing` in `books.json`, like the back's).
- **UV belakang** (`<book>-sampul-belakang-uv.png`): the blurb, the kicker at the foot and the glowing circle (`backUvMarkup`; the circle is an ellipse from `backRing` in `books.json`: center x, center y, width, height, in % of the cover, measured on `back.jpeg`).
- **UV punggung** (`<book>-punggung-uv.png`): the spine's stacked kicker, its title and SITI's portrait as a silhouette (`spineUvMarkup`).

The reader shows them as the last pages, laid out like the covers: the back's mask with the spine's to its right, then the front's on its own. Each is the cover's own layout with the pictures taken out and the coated parts in black (`.uv-mask` in `assets/base.css`). Where a circle and text overlap, both are black, so the UV covers them as one area. The circles are drawn by the page, so their edges are smooth. On the front, SITI comes from `ai/book-cover/web/front-uv-siti.png` (`uvArt` in `books.json`), with a smoothed outline and a soft edge.

SITI's outline is made by `tools/uv-mask.py` from two pictures, on this Mac (nothing is uploaded or generated). `ai/book-cover/front-siti-only.png` (the cover's SITI on white, everyone else removed) gives her rough outline: everything that isn't white, small white spots inside her filled, big gaps (between her legs) left open, minus her thin glow. That picture isn't an exact copy of the cover (her arm hangs a little apart), so OpenCV's GrabCut then snaps the outline to the real edges in `front.jpeg`, within a small band around it. That way the edge, and the gap between her hand and her skirt, follow the cover itself. The outline is then smoothed (`SMOOTH`, 2 px), with a soft (anti-aliased) edge, and its fingerprint goes in `web/index.json` so browsers load the new one. If the front picture changes, make a new SITI-only picture to match and rerun it (needs opencv-python-headless and numpy; the optional preview shows the outline in red over the cover):

```bash
python3 tools/uv-mask.py /tmp/uv-check.jpg
```

Ask the printer how they want the mask: many take this PNG, some want it as a spot color layer in a PDF. Text edges are smoothed (anti-aliased) in the PNG.

## Coloring book

The coloring book in `draw/` is a local work in progress: the folder is in `.gitignore`, so it is not in git or on the site, and it is not in `books.json` until it is ready.

## Deploy

Every push to `main` publishes the site to GitHub Pages with `.github/workflows/pages.yml` (plain static files, no build step). Only the picture copies in `web/` are in git; the originals stay local, so run `tools/covers.py` on this machine and commit the copies it writes.
