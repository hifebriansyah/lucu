# ai/: Kecerdasan Buatan, Kelakuan Asli

Book 1: stories about AI. The shared writing rules are in `../CLAUDE.md`. SITI appears in every story.

## Parts

- Bagian Satu: Jam Kerja (1–18)
- Bagian Dua: Di Rumah (19–30)
- Bagian Tiga: Satu RT (31–40)
- Bagian Empat: Politik (41–50)
- Bagian Lima: Rumah Tangga (51–60): married life
- Bagian Enam: Pinjaman Online (61–70)
- Epilog (71): "Nggak Bilang", the finale. It stays last.

## Cast in this book

- **SITI**: AI agent M4.KZ-A. Too obedient, too literal, too honest. Registered as a karyawan tetap at Pak Bos's office, and in a secret relationship with Pak Bos.
- **Pak Bos**: the office boss. Always arrives at eleven but claims eight, loves gorengan, has Pak Satpam do his titip absen.
- **Bu HRD**: HR at the office. Works late on Friday nights.
- **Dimas**: tired IT developer and Lead Developer. Secretly dating Nadia, later marries her (story 40). Lives in the same RT as Pak Darmo.
- **Raka**: got in with SITI's fake CV ("ahli IT senior, lima tahun di Silicon Valley"). His only real job was five years at Silicon Valley Cell, a phone-credit counter. Junior at the office, and in story 39 a fake lawyer. Neighbour in the same RT.
- **Bang Yudi**: fifteen-year programmer who claims he never uses AI, but secretly does. Three years single.
- **Nadia**: finance. Dating Dimas.
- **Pak Satpam**: outsourced security guard, so he never has to check in himself.
- **Pak Darmo**: former village dukun whose practice died because of SITI. Became a fake lawyer through SITI's CV. Borrows Bima's phone, watches coconut-water videos, pays a motorbike loan.
- **Bu Ratna**: Pak Darmo's wife, practical, unfairly suspected of overspending. RT treasurer.
- **Bima**: their son, in SD. Honest and smart, but keeps losing to adults who trust SITI.
- **Bu Guru**: Bima's teacher. Trusts SITI more than her students.
- **Pak Lurah**: head of the kelurahan, where SITI works a counter.
